## Scope and mode
Creator screen (`/[locale]/create`, `/[locale]/characters/[id]/edit`). Mode: **Operate**: the visitor customizes a character and finishes with "Bitti".

## Audience, task and constraints
- Management watches a live demo on a laptop; the presenter builds a character in one sitting.
- Task: name the character, then pick gender, face, body type and height, skin tone, eyes, hair, top, bottom, shoes, jewellery and tattoos, and finish.
- Constraints: every option sits in the LEFT panel (user requirement), the 3D character fills the rest, TR/EN, and nothing may look simple or templated.

## Chosen direction and memorable moment
- Direction: "The Premiere Wall". Each category is a horizontally scrolling row of 3:4 poster tiles inside a dark backstage panel; the focused tile grows, the active row lifts and the others dim.
- The camera glides to the focused category and a title card names it.
- Memorable moment: "Bitti" triggers the premiere. House lights dim, the stage light finds the character, and its name lands as a title card before chat opens.
